**Story Bank — How to Use**

1. Read `01` first. The coverage matrix tells you which story to reach for any question theme.
2. Practise each story aloud twice: once at the 90-second target, once as the 2-line short version.
3. Have 2-3 metric figures per story memorised cold. Interviewers can smell vagueness.
4. For every story, memorise the "Learning" sentence — bar-raiser rounds probe growth mindset hard.
5. After one full read, use `02` and `03` for leadership Q&A practice.

**Study order:** 01 → 02 → 03  
**Time to revise:** ~90 minutes (first read); ~30 minutes (re-revise on interview morning)
